02[s]inkworksink / ink

under the glass, a vanitas1 pours: skulls, cut flowers, soap bubbles, a still life that refuses to stay still.

1vanitas: latin for emptiness. painters in the 1600s set out skulls, cut flowers and soap bubbles to say that everything passes. of these, bubbles pass fastest.1a
1ahomo bulla, the old prints say: a person is a bubble. lately the word floats over a whole industry too. here they swell slowly, shine, and pop.
2.5 sbetween frames
128 x 96dots of light
size = lightone dot per patch of water

slow for a computer, about right for a tide pool. light arrives as dots, each one as large as its patch of water is bright.

at the center sits a black hole. it bends the grid round itself, and near its edge every dot's clock runs slow, then stops.2

2near a black hole, a clock watched from far away runs slow, then stops at the edge; light climbing out loses its blue. here the dots do both: slower near the drain, and rose.2a
2arose, not red. this water has no warm colours left. its gold went grey.

reach in. for about a second the water follows your hand, then slips through your fingers.
